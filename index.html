Project Selene

Protecting Children's Digital Footprints

Welcome to Project Selene

Project Selene is a digital privacy awareness website designed to help parents understand how sharing information about their children online can affect their safety and privacy.

Children's Digital Privacy Assessment

1. Do you share your child's full name online?

2. Do your photos or posts reveal your child's school?

3. Do you share your child's current location online?

4. Are your social media posts about your child visible to the public?

5. Do you share your child's daily routines or schedule online?
