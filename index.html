Protecting Children's Digital Footprints

Welcome to Project Selene

Project Selene is a digital privacy awareness website designed to help parents understand how sharing information about their children online can affect their safety and privacy.

Children's Digital Privacy Assessment

Answer the following 10 questions based on what you share about your child online. Each question includes examples to help you recognize information you may be sharing without realizing it.

This assessment is for educational purposes only. Your answers are not collected or saved.

1. Do you share pictures or posts that include your child's full name online?

This could include birthday announcements, school achievements, certificates, or posts where your child's first and last name are visible.

2. Have you shared pictures or posts that reveal which school or daycare your child attends?

For example, first-day-of-school pictures, school uniforms, building signs, backpacks with school logos, or posts mentioning the school's name.

3. Do you share your child's current location or tag places while you're still there?

This could include checking in at parks, restaurants, playgrounds, or events while you're visiting with your child.

4. Are pictures or posts about your child visible to people outside your trusted friends and family?

For example, public Facebook posts, public Instagram accounts, or TikTok videos that anyone can view, share, or save.

5. Do you share information about your child's daily schedule or places they regularly visit?

This might include school drop-off and pickup times, daycare schedules, weekly activities, or locations your child visits on certain days.

6. Do you share your child's exact birthday, age, or grade level on social media?

For example, birthday posts showing their full date of birth, first-day-of-school signs, or updates about what grade they're entering.

7. Have you shared pictures that could reveal where your child lives?

This could include house numbers, street signs, recognizable landmarks, or pictures showing the outside of your home.

8. Do you post pictures that show your family's vehicle or license plate?

For example, pictures taken in the driveway, family outings, or photos where a license plate or other identifying vehicle details are clearly visible.

9. Do you share vacation pictures or travel updates while your family is still away from home?

This might include announcing when you're leaving, posting your hotel location, or sharing that your family will be away for several days.

10. Do you share pictures or information that reveal where your child regularly participates in activities?

For example, sports team names, dance studios, practice schedules, team uniforms, or locations where your child attends weekly activities.
